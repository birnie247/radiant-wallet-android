Lumen RXD Wallet

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

The short version: Lumen RXD Wallet is a self-custody cryptocurrency wallet. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no backend. Its only network traffic goes to ElectrumX servers — including a default one run by the developer, which sees your IP and the addresses you query, and which you can replace in Settings. Your recovery phrase is created and encrypted on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

1. Who this applies to

This policy covers the Lumen RXD Wallet Android application (com.rxdexplorer.lumen), published by Birnie ("we", "us"). It applies only to that app; it does not cover websites or services you reach from it.

2. What we collect

Nothing. The app has no backend: no accounts, no analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or advertising SDKs, and no identifier for you or your device. We never receive your recovery phrase, your private keys, your balances or your passphrase.

One nuance, because leaving it out would be dishonest: the app ships with a default ElectrumX endpoint operated by the developer (wss://rxdexplorer.com). Like any Electrum server it sees your IP address and the blockchain addresses the wallet queries — nothing else, and never a key or the phrase. Prefer not to use it? Settings → ElectrumX server lets you switch to another server or add your own, and the choice is remembered on the device.

3. Data stored on your device

DataWhere and why
Your recovery phrase (seed) Encrypted with AES-256-GCM under a key derived from your passphrase (PBKDF2-SHA256, 600,000 iterations) and stored in the app's private storage. If you enable biometric unlock, a second copy is encrypted by a hardware-backed key in the Android Keystore.
Preferences The Electrum server you picked, whether screenshots are allowed, your auto-lock delay, and whether USD values are shown — in the app's private storage. The most recent RXD/USD price is cached there too, so the balance can show a value without a fresh request.

All of this stays on your device. Uninstalling the app, or using "Delete wallet" in Settings, removes it. We cannot recover it for you — only your written recovery phrase can.

4. Network connections

To show balances and broadcast transactions, the app talks directly to public ElectrumX servers — community operators, the Radiant project, and the developer-run default endpoint (rxdexplorer.com) — over encrypted WebSocket connections.

Those servers necessarily see your IP address and the blockchain addresses you query. That is inherent to how lightweight wallets work, and it is why the app supports switching servers. For the developer-run default endpoint, this policy is the one that applies; the others are governed by their own policies. The app does not send your recovery phrase, keys, or passphrase to any server.

USD prices (optional). If you leave Show USD value enabled (Settings → Display), the app fetches the RXD/USD rate from a public price API — CoinGecko, falling back to CoinPaprika. That request tells the provider your IP address and that you use a Radiant wallet; it carries no wallet data, addresses or balance. It is sent at most once every couple of minutes, and the result is cached on your device. Turn the setting off and the app makes no price requests at all.

5. Permissions

PermissionWhy
INTERNET To reach public ElectrumX servers.
USE_BIOMETRIC Only if you turn on biometric unlock, to unlock the wallet with your fingerprint or face. Biometric data is handled by Android and is never available to the app.

The app requests no location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage access, and collects no advertising identifiers.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 18.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export, or delete on request. You exercise full control locally: view or export your recovery phrase in Settings, and delete all wallet data with "Delete wallet" or by uninstalling.
